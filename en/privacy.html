This website doesn't collect personal information on its own. It has no analytics, no advertising cookies and no contact form that stores data.

When you write to us

The demo request opens your own email app with a message you can edit. We receive only what you send, and use it to build your demo and reply. We don't sell or share it.

How long we keep it

Demo requests and emails are kept while we work together and for up to two years after, then deleted. Ask us and we'll delete them sooner.

Changes and questions
