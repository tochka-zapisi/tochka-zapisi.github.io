Good to know

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes, when a chair is free. Booking online guarantees your time, especially Friday and Saturday.

How do I cancel or move my appointment?

Reply to the reminder text or call us. Please let us know at least 2 hours ahead so someone else can take the slot.

Do you cut kids' hair?

Every day we're open. Kids 12 and under get the Kids Cut price.

How do I pay?

Card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or cash. Tips can go on the card.

Is the shop accessible?

Step-free entrance from Alder Street and an accessible restroom. Call ahead if you need anything else and we'll set it up.
