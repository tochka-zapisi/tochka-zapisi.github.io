Questions we hear every week.

Do you take my insurance?

We are in network with most PPO plans and file claims for you. Send us your plan name before the first visit and we'll check your coverage.

What if I don't have insurance?

Fees on this page are our cash prices. We also offer an in-house plan with two cleanings a year and 15% off treatment.

I haven't been to a dentist in years.

That's common. The first visit is an exam and a conversation, and we only start treatment after you've seen the plan and the price.

What about emergencies?

Call before 10 AM on weekdays and we'll find a same-day chair. After hours, the voicemail gives Dr. Ashby's number.

Is the office accessible?

Ground-floor entrance from the parking lot with no steps, wide doors, and an accessible restroom. Tell us if you need extra time in the chair.
