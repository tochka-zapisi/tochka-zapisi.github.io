Accessibility Statement

We want everyone to be able to book a visit and find the information they need on this website.

Our standard

This site is designed to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA. It works with keyboard navigation and screen readers, respects your device's reduced-motion setting, and uses text with sufficient color contrast.

At the shop

The entrance is step-free and the lobby has room for a wheelchair. Call ahead and we'll bring your dog out to the car.

Something not working?

If any part of this website is hard to use, call us at (713) 555-0148 or email hello@studewoodgrooming.example and we will help you book by phone and fix the issue. We review this site regularly.
