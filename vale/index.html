Questions, answered.

Is the consultation really free?

Yes. Thirty minutes with a provider and a written plan with prices. No obligation to book a treatment.

Who performs injections?

Licensed nurse practitioners, under the supervision of our medical director, Dr. Ellison.

How long do results last?

It depends on the treatment and on you. Your provider will explain typical ranges at the consultation and book a check-in.

What is your cancellation policy?

Please give us 24 hours' notice. Reply to your reminder text or call the concierge line.

Is the studio accessible?

Step-free entrance from the parking lot, a wide doorway into every room and an accessible restroom.
