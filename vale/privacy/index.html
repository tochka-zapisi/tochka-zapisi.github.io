Privacy Policy

This policy explains what Vale Skin Studio PLLC (“we”) collects through this website and how we use it.

What we collect

When you book an appointment we ask for your name, phone number and, if you choose, your email address and a note. We use them only to confirm, remind you about and manage your appointment.

Text messages

If you check the box to receive text reminders, we will send appointment confirmations and reminders to the number you provide. Message frequency varies; message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, HELP for help. We never send marketing texts without separate consent.

What we don't do

We do not sell or share your personal information for advertising. This site does not use advertising cookies.

Who processes your data

Your booking request is delivered to us through our booking provider and stored only as long as needed to provide the service and meet legal requirements.

California residents

Under the CCPA/CPRA you may request to know, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you. We do not sell or share personal information. To make a request, contact us using the details below. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. Parents book appointments for kids.
